INTERVIEW PREP WORKSPACE

Go in prepared.

A simple framework for building confidence before each interview.

⌕

Research the company

Know the business before you join the conversation.

  • Read the company website
  • Understand its product and users
  • Review the job description
  • Find recent company news
✦

Prepare your story

Use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

  • Introduce yourself in 60 seconds
  • Describe one project clearly
  • Prepare a challenge you solved
  • Explain what you learned
?

Ask thoughtful questions

Good questions show curiosity and maturity.

  • What does success look like?
  • What will I learn in this role?
  • How does the team collaborate?
  • What are the next steps?
PRACTICE QUESTION

“Tell me about a project you are proud of.”

Use your Trackly project: explain the problem, the pages you built, secure login, database, and what you would improve next.
